Introduces the 4D AI Fluency framework — four interconnected competencies for effective,
efficient, ethical, and safe AI collaboration: **Delegation** (setting goals and deciding
when to engage AI), **Description** (communicating goals so the AI behaves usefully),
**Discernment** (assessing whether AI output is actually good), and **Diligence** (taking
responsibility for what you do with AI). It also names three modes of interaction —
Automation, Augmentation, and Agency.

No external links on these slides; the framework is the throughline the workshop returns to.
