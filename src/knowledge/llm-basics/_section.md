Explains how large language models work at a practical level: the pipeline from raw data →
tokens → the transformer architecture → pre-training → post-training → serving/inference.
Covers context windows, tokenization, and why GPU acceleration matters for both training and
serving. The goal is enough mental model to reason about model behavior and limits, not a
math-heavy deep dive.

No external links appear on these slides; the content is conceptual background for the rest of
the workshop.
