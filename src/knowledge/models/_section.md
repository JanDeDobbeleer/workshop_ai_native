Surveys the model landscape and how to choose the right AI coding assistant, using public
benchmarks as one input among several. Explains what the leaderboards measure and their limits.

Source links:
- **SWE-bench** (swebench.com): a benchmark of real GitHub issues; the "Verified" leaderboard
  is the common reference for coding-agent capability.
- **BenchLM HumanEval**: results for the classic HumanEval code-generation benchmark.
- **arXiv:2505.09598** ("How Hungry is AI?", May 2025): on the energy/resource cost of running
  models, relevant to the trade-offs of larger context and bigger models.
