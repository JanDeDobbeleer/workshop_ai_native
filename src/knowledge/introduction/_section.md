Opens the workshop and frames the AI-native developer mindset: treating AI not as an
occasional autocomplete but as a collaborator you delegate to, direct, and supervise. It
sets up the core challenge the rest of the deck addresses — adoption is uneven, and getting
value from AI is a skill (context, prompting, tooling) rather than a switch you flip.

The linked repository is the source for this very slide deck, an interactive React/TypeScript
slideshow used to deliver the workshop.
