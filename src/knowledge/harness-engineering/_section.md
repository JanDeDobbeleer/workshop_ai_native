The "harness" is the layer around the model — the repo, hooks, gates, and feedback loops that
turn a raw LLM into a reliable agent. Key ideas: the repo is the system of record ("if the
agent can't see it, it doesn't exist"), mechanical enforcement plus feedback hooks (checks that
always run), and safety gates, context, and cleanup. Illustrated with oh-my-posh stop hooks.

Source: **harness-engineering.ai** — the guide referenced on the slides (OpenAI, "Harness
engineering", Feb 2026).
