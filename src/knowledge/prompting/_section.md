Covers prompt engineering techniques while arguing the term itself is in decline — as models
and tooling improve, raw wording matters less than good context and iteration. Includes
model-driven refinement (letting the model improve your prompt), meta-prompting, and
self-analysis loops where the model critiques and adjusts its own approach.

Source: "Stop speedrunning Claude Code" (paddo.dev) — on slowing down and being deliberate with
agentic coding instead of rushing prompts.
