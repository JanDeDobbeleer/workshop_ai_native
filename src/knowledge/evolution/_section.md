Traces how software development is changing with AI across eight stages — from simple code
completions, through trust-building with permission-based agents, CLI and multi-agent work,
and into an "orchestration era" where a developer coordinates many agents at once. The arc is
a shift from typing code to directing and reviewing work produced by AI.

Source: Steve Yegge's "Welcome to Gas Town" (January 2026), a widely cited essay on the
messy, fast-moving reality of agent-heavy development.
