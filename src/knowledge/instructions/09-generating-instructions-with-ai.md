Use AI to generate and refine custom instructions from your repository's existing patterns and
style. VS Code has a built-in generator: **Configure Chat → Generate Chat Instructions** — it
analyzes the codebase automatically, creates a starting point from detected patterns, and then
you review and customize. For meta-prompting techniques, continuous improvement strategies, and
advanced prompt refinement, the deck points to the **Prompting** section.
