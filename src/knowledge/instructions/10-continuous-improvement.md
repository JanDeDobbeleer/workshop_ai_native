Treat instructions as living documents refined through a feedback loop. After generating
output, ask the model to explain its deviations and adjust the instructions for future tasks.
The process: review outputs for deviations, ask the model to analyze the issues, and update
instructions iteratively. This yields instructions that evolve with your needs, reduced
code-review friction, and more consistent AI output. Example feedback prompt: "Go through our
conversation and iterations we made and implement/adjust instructions to avoid these mistakes in
the future." Good instructions improve through real-world usage and feedback.
