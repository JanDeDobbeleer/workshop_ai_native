title: Why MCP Matters
tags: mcp, integration, m-times-n, standard, ecosystem
---
MCP solves the integration-complexity problem by turning M×N into M+N. Without a standard,
every model needs a custom connector for every tool (M models × N tools). With MCP, models and
tools each conform to one protocol, so you only build M + N adapters. The benefits split three
ways: developers get reduced build time and complexity; AI apps get access to a whole ecosystem
of data sources, tools, and apps; and end users get more capable AI that can reach their data
and take actions.
